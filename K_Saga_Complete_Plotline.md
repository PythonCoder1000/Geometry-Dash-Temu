# 1. THE ORIGINAL PREMISE

The story began as a deliberately absurd debate comedy about nuclear power.

The original debaters were intentionally bad at reasoning. They were stubborn, emotionally committed to their sides, and capable of producing arguments so terrible that even when they accidentally reached a correct conclusion, they usually did it for the wrong reason.

The central running conflict became:

- **Derek** = pro-nuclear.
- **Mason** = anti-nuclear or anti-Derek, depending on which Mason version is involved.

The story then escalated from dumb energy arguments into increasingly impossible debaters, supercomputers, truth-altering beings, impossible chambers, fake realities, multiversal prisons, and finally a multiverse where nearly everyone has universe-specific counterparts.

Despite all of this, one recurring constant survived every escalation:

**Mason says stupid things about nuclear water.**

# 2. EARLY CHARACTER ESCALATION

## 2.1 Mason 1

**Mason 1** is the original ridiculous debater.

He is not particularly intelligent compared with the later cast, but he is extremely stubborn and has a unique talent for producing arguments that irritate Derek.

Recurring Mason 1 reasoning includes ideas such as:

- “Nuclear water.”
- “Steam is smoke.”
- “Trees are construction vegetables.”
- “Clouds are pollution.”
- “If nuclear takes ten years, build it ten years earlier.”
- “If a reactor creates industrial heat, the factory is nuclear.”
- “Storage is a time battery.”
- “A permit taking five years can be solved by submitting it five years earlier.”
- “Water from nuclear” as a definition of nuclear water.
- Treating any vaguely nuclear-associated object as if it becomes “nuclear.”
- Treating terminology as if labels change physics.
- Calling knights “horses” in chess and refusing to care about the distinction.

The important thing about Mason 1 is that his reasoning is often so internally unstable that later, when the cast confronts an entity capable of attacking definitions and logical coherence, Mason’s nonsense becomes strangely resistant to semantic attacks.

He is not secretly a genius.

He is simply difficult to model because his arguments are terrible.
